---
title: Plans and versions
description: "Keep commercial rules stable while you change pricing."
---

A plan is the commercial offer that you sell. A plan version is the package of that offer:
billing settings, features, meters, included quantities, limits, and one rate card per currency.
The rate card holds the prices.

## Plan: stable identity

Use the plan for the name and stable slug of an offer, such as `pro`. The plan slug selects the
latest published version during customer signup.

## Plan version: commercial rules

Use a plan version for the rules that must remain true for a customer after you publish them. A
version can contain a billing period, features, usage meters, included quantities, limits, and
credit policy. It stores no price: it names the rate card that prices it in each currency.

## Rate card: the prices

A rate card is a list of price rows. Each row prices one feature, or one combination of a feature's
dimensions, such as one `model`.

- A row never changes. To change a price, publish a new row and end the old row in the same
  publish, where the new one starts.
- Once a card has rows, a new row or a row end takes effect at least 10 minutes after you publish
  it. Only a card's first rows can start at once. Customer budgets refresh their cards at least
  every 5 minutes, so no usage is priced with a price that has ended.
- A change to a card's rows reaches every customer whose plan version uses that card. To keep
  existing customers on their price, put the new price on a new card in a new plan version.
- Unprice prices usage once, when it accepts the event. Invoices add up those amounts and never
  price usage again. Recurring fees, such as a base fee or seats, are priced from the card when the
  billing period closes.
- Usage of a priced feature that no row matches is rejected with `NOT_PRICED`. It is never priced
  at zero.

## Lifecycle

| State | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | The version is still being configured. | Review its rules before you publish it. |
| Published | The version can be assigned to customers. Publishing refuses a version with a feature its rate card does not price. | Use it for signup or migration. |
| Archived | The version is not available for new assignment. | Keep it as evidence for customers who used it. |

## Change a commercial rule

To change a price for every customer on a card, publish a new row on the card.

To change an entitlement, meter, limit, or billing rule, or to change a price for some customers
only:

1. Create a draft from the current plan.
2. Change the rule, or point the draft at a different rate card.
3. Review and publish the new version.
4. Assign or migrate only the customers who should receive the new terms.

This model avoids a request-path branch such as `if (plan === "pro")`. Your product checks a stable
feature slug. Unprice resolves the customer's plan version and entitlement state.

Read [Feature types](/concepts/pricing/feature-types) and [Usage metering](/concepts/pricing/usage-metering)
before you publish a usage-based version.
