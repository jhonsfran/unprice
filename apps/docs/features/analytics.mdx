---
title: 'Analytics'
description: 'Usage, spend, and invoice evidence for a customer money path.'
---

Use Analytics to inspect the evidence behind customer usage and spend. It helps you answer what
happened, which customer it affected, and which plan version and billing period applied.

## What to inspect

- **Usage:** Metered events and period totals for a customer or feature.
- **Spend:** Priced usage and wallet movement for the same customer money path.
- **Ingestion state:** Whether an event was processed, rejected, failed, or can be replayed.
- **Invoice evidence:** The plan, usage, wallet, and billing facts that explain an invoice line.

## Use evidence before changing pricing

Do not use a revenue chart as the only reason to change a plan. First check the request-path
decisions, usage pattern, wallet movement, and invoice evidence. Then create a new plan version
for the change. Existing customers can remain on the version that they bought.
