## Users

Unprice serves founders, CTOs, founding engineers, and platform engineers building AI products that
sell credits, agent runs, or paid workflows. They own customer budgets, wallets, usage settlement,
and invoice evidence.

## Product purpose

Unprice is open-source billing for AI credits and agent usage. It helps teams authorize workloads
before they create cost, reserve customer credits, settle actual usage, and trace invoice lines to
the decisions that allowed the work.

## Brand personality

Precise, open, fast, calm, and opinionated. The product should feel like trustworthy operational
infrastructure: technical enough for developers, legible enough for founders, and transparent
enough for money-adjacent workflows.

## Anti-references

Avoid black-box billing-tool aesthetics, vague growth-platform language, decorative gradients,
purple AI cliches, glossy marketing dashboards, and product screens that hide operational state
behind vanity metrics.

## Design principles

1. Show the customer money path from request to plan version, meter, entitlement, budget, wallet,
   and invoice evidence.
2. Prefer calm density: compact, consistent, token-driven UI over decorative emphasis.
3. Make state explicit with concrete lifecycle labels and obvious next actions.
4. Keep developer actions and identifiers close to the state they affect.
5. Use visual emphasis only when it changes a decision.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and form labeling. Respect
reduced motion. Do not rely on color alone for pricing, entitlement, success, warning, danger, or
failure states.
